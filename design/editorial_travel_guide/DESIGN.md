## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from high-end print journalism, architectural journals, and literary travel anthologies. It rejects the hyper-saturated, gamified patterns typical of mass-market online travel agencies in favor of quiet authority, deliberate rhythm, and tactile typography.

The interface serves discerning travelers seeking boutique accommodations, architectural retreats, and deeply contextual cultural itineraries. The emotional signature is unhurried, cultured, and scholarly yet deeply welcoming—evoking the tangible intimacy of heavy stock paper, linotype typesetting, and physical broadsheets. 

The aesthetic is purely editorial:
- **Zero skeuomorphic depth or synthetic blurs**: No drop shadows, floating elevations, or glassmorphic overlays.
- **Physical framing**: Space, typographic scale, and structural hairlines organize density and narrative sequence.
- **Asymmetric broadsheet cadence**: Layouts juxtapose expansive photographic margins with condensed multi-column details.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unbleached paper fibers, mineral inks, and architectural pigments.

### Foundations & Surfaces
- **Page Background (`#F7F4EF`)**: Warm bone paper. Sets the foundational canvas temperature.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFDFA`)**: Near-white warm tone. Reserved for cards, elevated editorial containers, and active inputs.
- **Inset / Substrate (`#EFEAE1`)**: Muted fiber tint. Applied to table alternating rows, structural divider strips, code blocks, and loading states.
- **Ink Panel (`#1F1B16`)**: Deep warm black ink. Applied to comprehensive footers, focal editorial takeovers, and high-impact structural blocks.

### Borders & Dividers
- **Hairline Border (`#DED7CA`)**: Architectural structural line used for cards, content separators, and section grid divisions.
- **Input Border at Rest (`#B9AE9C`)**: Mid-tone warm stone for distinct interactive affordance.

### Typography & Text
- **Primary Ink (`#1F1B16`)**: Dense black ink with brown undertones.
- **Secondary Ink (`#6B635A`)**: Weathered carbon for captions, secondary meta, and subtitles.
- **Placeholder Ink (`#918878`)**: Balanced neutral for empty input states and muted micro-labels.

### Accents & Semantics
- **Accent Solid (`#A63D22`)**: Deep mineral terracotta. Reserved strictly for primary action fills, badges, and key interactive highlights.
- **Accent Hover (`#8E3319`)**: Compressed rust ink for direct hover interactions.
- **Accent Display Text (`#C9522F`)**: Vibrated terracotta for large headlines and display-scale hero titles on warm bone paper.
- **Accent Wash (`#F6E7E1`)**: Pale terracotta bath for alert banners, selected table rows, or thematic highlights.
- **Editorial Link / Info (`#1F4E5F`)**: Deep maritime teal for inline editorial references, links, and secondary interactive metadata.
- **Link Hover (`#163B48`)**: Deepened spruce ink.
- **Success (`#3F6B4A`)**: Laurel green for confirmed bookings, availability, and affirmative states.
- **Warning (`#8A6A1F`)**: Raw umber gold for inventory thresholds and advisory callouts.
- **Danger (`#8C2F22`)**: Burnt crimson for system errors and destructive confirmations.

### Strict Contrast Rules
- Whenever terracotta (`#A63D22`) is used as a solid fill, typography must strictly render in `#FFFDFA`.
- Terracotta text tokens (`#C9522F`) must never be used below 24px.

## Typography

The type system mirrors classic European literary publishing.

### Font Roles
- **Editorial Display & Headings (`Newsreader`)**: High-contrast, expressive serif with historical rigor. Used for property titles, narrative features, pull quotes, and section markers.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Archivo Narrow`)**: Efficient, structured grotesque providing balanced legibility in dense travel itineraries, amenity matrices, and interactive forms.
- **Technical & Tabular Data (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, monospaced rendering for reference codes, geographic coordinates, flight paths, and programmatic data.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tabular Figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`)**: Mandatory on all price values, date blocks, currency strings, room dimensions, and availability meters to maintain clean alignment.
- **Uppercase Metadata Labels**: Micro-labels, kicker headings, badges, and card categories use `label-caps` set in all caps with `0.08em` tracking.
- **Editorial Quotes**: Quotation marks must use standard serif glyphs; avoid generic vertical strokes.
- **Body Text**: Left-aligned without exception. Never justify editorial copy.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric newspaper grid that embraces open space as an active framing element.

### Layout Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12-column layout with fixed margins of `4rem` (`64px`) and gutters of `2.5rem` (`40px`). Maximal content boundary caps at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` (`32px`) margins and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.

### Editorial Asymmetry & Rhythm
- Major hotel discovery modules pair wide narrative imagery (spanning 7 or 8 columns) with narrow architectural briefing panels (spanning 4 or 5 columns).
- Vertical pacing relies on alternating between high-density metadata matrices and expansive narrative whitespace (`space-xl` or larger).
- Structural section separators are delineated by horizontal hairlines with explicit `space-lg` top and bottom clearance.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic depth, skeuomorphic volume, and atmospheric drop shadows. Depth is established purely through physical division, surface tone, and structural boundaries.

### Flat Surface Architecture
- **No Drop Shadows**: Cards, modal dialogs, flyout drawers, floating toolbars, and action buttons have zero box-shadow properties.
- **Hairline Matrix**: Structural separation uses a consistent `1px solid #DED7CA` border. All adjacent cards, image containers, and layout sections lock together through shared or contiguous borders, evoking traditional lead-ruled print composition.
- **Tonal Stepping**: 
  - Ground layer: Bone paper (`#F7F4EF`).
  - Active focal items: Card surface (`#FFFDFA`) bordered with `#DED7CA`.
  - Recessed context: Substrate inset (`#EFEAE1`).
  - Anchored emphasis: Ink panel (`#1F1B16`) with hairline dividers rendered in `#38322B`.

## Shapes

The geometry is defined by clean, square architectural cuts:

- **Square Foundations (0px)**: All editorial cards, contextual image blocks, modal windows, table frames, dropdown sheets, and media viewports have sharp, unrounded corners (`0px`).
- **Constrained Interactive Corners (Max 2px)**: Primary buttons, text inputs, segmented pills, and system controls may use a subtle `2px` corner radius to soften sharp edge clipping on interactive states without sacrificing architectural precision.
- **Status Badges (Pill Shape Only)**: Full border-radius pills (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for compact, inline status indicators (e.g., "Available", "Booked", "Curator's Pick").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#A63D22` background, strictly `#FFFDFA` text, `2px` radius. Hover shifts to `#8E3319`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Archivo Narrow 600 weight.
- **Secondary / Outline**: `#FFFDFA` background, `1px solid #1F1B16` border, `#1F1B16` text. Hover shifts to `#F7F4EF`.
- **Text / Editorial Action**: Transparent fill, `#1F4E5F` text with a permanent `1px` underline offset by `3px`. Hover shifts to `#163B48`.

### Inputs & Date Selectors
- **Container**: Surface `#FFFDFA`, `1px solid #B9AE9C`, `2px` radius.
- **Height**: `44px` tall with horizontal padding of `0.75rem`.
- **States**: Focus transition triggers a `1px solid #1F1B16` perimeter with no outer glow rings. Error states trigger `1px solid #8C2F22`.
- **Labels**: Rendered above inputs using `label-caps` in `#6B635A`.

### Cards & Property Teasers
- **Base**: Grounded on `#FFFDFA`, bound by a crisp `1px solid #DED7CA` line, strictly `0px` radius.
- **Images**: Fixed aspect ratio (typically 3:2 or 4:5), sharp `0px` corners, separated from text content below by a `1px solid #DED7CA` line.
- **Meta Content**: Hotel category or location tags set in `label-caps` in `#6B635A`, listing title in `headline-sm`, and prices right-aligned using tabular figures in JetBrains Mono.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: Border radius of `9999px`. Height: `22px`. Padding: `0.125rem 0.625rem`.
- **Curator Pick**: Solid `#1F1B16` with `#FFFDFA` text.
- **Availability / Confirmed**: Pale laurel wash background (`#E8EFEA`) with `#3F6B4A` text.
- **Limited Inventory**: Accent wash (`#F6E7E1`) with `#A63D22` text.

### Tables & Amenity Matrices
- **Dividers**: Every row features a bottom border of `1px solid #DED7CA`.
- **Alternating Rows**: Optional `#EFEAE1` tint for dense room inventory tables.
- **Headers**: Clean `#F7F4EF` fill, uppercase `label-caps` text left-aligned, tabular data headers right-aligned.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Geometry**: Checkboxes are `16px x 16px` with `0px` radius; radio buttons are circular.
- **Frame**: `1px solid #B9AE9C` border on `#FFFDFA`. Checked state fills with solid `#A63D22` and sharp white iconography.

### Editorial Additions
- **Folio Header / Breadcrumb**: Top navigational masthead separated by a horizontal `1px solid #DED7CA`, featuring section classifications in tracked uppercase text.
- **Architectural Spec Box**: An inset callout using `#EFEAE1` surface with a `1px solid #DED7CA` perimeter, summarizing room square meters, sun orientation, and construction year in JetBrains Mono.
