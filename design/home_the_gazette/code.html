ARTICLE IV · EDITORIAL INTEGRITY

The Standard of Inclusion

A hotel is not merely a bed inside a container; it is an author’s commentary on its surrounding geography. We accept less than six percent of nominated addresses.

PROPRIETOR COVENANT

Lodgings are unannouncedly visited twice every 24 months. Failure to maintain acoustic quietude or genuine material standard results in immediate de-registration.

§ 01

Acoustic & Luminary Sovereignty

Total black-out capability, acoustic decibel thresholds under 32dB at night, and zero synthetic blue ambient LED standby lamps.

§ 02

Architectural Provenance

Preservation of original stone, lime plaster, or period timber. No mock finishes, vinyl veneers, or generic interior contractor packages.

§ 03

Independent Governance

Properties must be privately held or architect-led. Publicly traded hospitality conglomerates and fractional timeshares are excluded by statute.

§ 04

Zero Placement Economy

Neither hotelier nor regional tourism council may remit currency for ranking or listing. Inspection travel is funded solely through reader subscription.
